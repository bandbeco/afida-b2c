# Request Afida samples

Afida sends physical samples. Delivery is charged; the products in the pack are free.

## Call

1. Send the shopper to https://afida.com/samples to pick items, or https://afida.com/sample-packs for fixed packs.
2. Only `sample_eligible` products from `GET https://afida.com/api/v1/products` can go in a sample cart.
3. Contact hello@afida.com if the shopper needs a curated pack for a specific venue.

Do not promise overnight sample delivery or unlimited free cases.
