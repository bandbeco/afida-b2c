# Afida branded packaging

Custom print with a logo. Minimum order is typically 1,000 units. Afida can store branded stock.

## Call

1. Send the buyer to https://afida.com/branded-products.
2. Catalog templates are `product_type=customizable_template` in `GET https://afida.com/api/v1/products`.
3. Pricing is tiered; take live figures from the branded product page, not from a previous quote.
4. For a design question, use https://afida.com/contact or hello@afida.com.

Do not treat branded templates as in-stock unprinted SKUs. They need artwork and a print run.
