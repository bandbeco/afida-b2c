## 2. W1 category retitles: no click effect at 4 weeks

View: page contains `/categories/`, all 37 rows read.

- **Clicks 27 down to 15** (8.7k imp, avg pos 24.6). The retitles have not lifted category clicks; the surface actually fell.
- The wholesale-modifier bucket the retitles target is still clickless: query contains wholesale 0 clicks / 141 imp (July: 0 / 969), bulk 0 / 133, supplier 0 / 336, catering 1 / 485. First-ever bucket click ("starting a catering business uk", a blog term). The impression collapse in "wholesale" (969 to 141) looks like AI fan-out receding, not rankings moving.
- Position bright spots exist under the flat clicks: straws pos 32.8 to 21.4, hot-cups pos 4.6 (45 imp), plates-and-bowls pos 8.0, cup-accessories 14.9. The retitle doc's caveat stands: cutlery (49.2) and straws are too far back for titles alone, and that is how it played out.
- `vegware cups` moved pos 23.3 to 18.0 and earned its first click; `vegware` flat at 21.1; `vegware uk` sits at pos 9.0 with 130 imp and 0 clicks. `natureflex bags` DECLINED 11.7 to 14.6.

**Verdict: no measurable click effect in 4 weeks.** Retitles are CTR levers, and category pages still hold too few page-1 positions for a title to matter. Do not extend or iterate the retitles; the constraint is position, not title copy.

## 5. Category-rename equity: consolidated, at a lower level

View: the 37-row `/categories/` page table (old and new URLs share the prefix).

- **Index consolidation is complete.** The old renamed URLs have dropped out: of ~18 old paths, only three still register at all (old cup-accessories 9 imp, deli-pots 6 imp, plates-and-trays 2 imp). In July the old ice-cream-cups URL alone carried 12 clicks / 2,814 imp.
- The new URLs absorbed the impressions: ice-cream-cups 1,907 imp (July: 826 new + 2,814 old), soup-containers 887 (786 + 254).
- **But the consolidated pages perform below the old URLs' peaks**: new ice-cream-cups is 4 clicks / pos 14.6 vs the old URL's 12 clicks / pos 9.8 in July, and the whole category surface fell 27 to 15 clicks. Google has merged the URLs without (yet) granting the new ones the old rankings.

**Verdict: transfer complete, equity partially lost.** There is no further action available; the 301s and slug history are correct, and this is the known cost of the June renames playing out. Watch ice-cream-cups (the highest-volume pair) at the next pull; if pos 14.6 does not close toward ~10, the loss is permanent and the page needs an on-page reason to re-rank.
