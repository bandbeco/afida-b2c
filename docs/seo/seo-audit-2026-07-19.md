## 1. Headline: organic clicks are back at the pre-rename baseline; the dramatic Datafa.st dip was not organic search

Two sources, two stories, now reconciled:

**GSC (web search, daily clicks):** ~11/day Jun 22-26, ~6.9/day Jun 27-Jul 5 (the measured ~31% rename damage), then a steady climb to **~10.4/day over Jul 6-17**. The B2B plan's watchdog ("daily clicks back above the pre-rename ~10/day baseline") is satisfied: recovered to baseline, not above it. Average position is stable (12.6-14). Daily impressions are trending down (2,900 → 1,600/day over the window), but impressions are a polluted KPI (AI fan-out, see §7).

**Datafa.st "Google" referrer (visitors/day):** a much deeper V: 68-98/day pre-dip, 17-43/day Jul 1-13, then 90-102/day by Jul 17-19. GSC proves organic web clicks never moved anywhere near that amplitude, so most of this swing is **non-organic Google traffic bundled under the same referrer** (Google Ads/PMax clicks carry a google.com referrer; googlesyndication/doubleclick appear separately but search ads don't). Read: the mid-July traffic crater and recovery in Datafa.st is largely a paid-traffic story (budget, pause, or serving change worth checking in the Ads account), not an SEO event. The `seo-phase-0` redirect deploy (Jul 8) plus reindex requests likely drove the modest organic click recovery visible in GSC from Jul 6-17.

## 5. New findings from this audit

1. **Merchant feed taxonomy gap.** The feed (649 items, all active products, GTIN/price/availability/image all present) maps `google_product_category` from a hand-keyed slug map that predates the June renames: `bowls-and-lids`, `portion-pots-and-lids` (at least) are absent, so their products ship without a Google product category. Merchant listings are the site's highest-CTR surface (26.7% per the 07-07 export); this is a cheap fix in `google_merchant_feed_generator.rb`.
2. **Outrank pile-up accelerating: 89 drafts** (31 in May, 82 on Jul 7, +7 in 12 days). The B4 decision (gate or kill the subscription) is overdue.
3. **Returning-visitor share doubled** (10.3% → 19.0%). Worth watching: if it holds it suggests the trade/repeat audience the whole plan targets is materialising.
4. **robots.txt actively welcomes AI crawlers** (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot etc. plus `Content-Signal: ai-train=yes`) and ChatGPT referrals continue (~29/mo). GEO groundwork is in place; rendering the question-H2s is the remaining free win.
5. `/categories/hot-food/food-containers` shadowed-slug exception survives as a documented static route; fine, but remember it exists if hot-food is ever renamed again.

## 7. GSC measurement checkpoint (28 days to 2026-07-17, web search)

Headline numbers: **251 clicks / 68,120 impressions / 28 days** (~9.0 clicks/day). Brand ("afida" queries) is 31 clicks, ~12% of the total; non-brand now carries the site. Clicks by surface: blog 117 (47%), products 51, homepage 35, categories 27, collections 10, other 12.

**404 recovery and equity transfer.** Clicks are back at the ~10/day pre-rename baseline (§1), but the equity transfer to the new URLs is incomplete: the old `/categories/cups-and-drinks/ice-cream-cups` still earns **12 clicks / 2,814 imp at pos 9.8** while the new `cups-and-accessories` URL gets 4 clicks / 826 imp at pos 13.9. Same pattern on soup-containers (old 254 imp, new 786 imp, both 0 clicks, pos ~25) and the vegware filter pages (old `cups-and-drinks` variant 158 imp vs new 83). Users are fine (all 301), but Google is still splitting signals across old/new; expect consolidation over the coming weeks now the redirects are DB-driven.

**B1 gate verdict (buying-guide batch 2): FAIL, stay paused.** The rule was "≥1 click gained AND ≥3 positions" on restaurants or coffee-shops. `/collections/coffee-shops` finally earns clicks (5 in 28 days at pos 11.3, vs 4 per quarter at 07-07 and 0 in May), but its position is flat, and `/collections/restaurants` is still 0 clicks at pos 15.1. The coffee-shops click uptick is the one positive Move 2 signal; it argues for internal links and retitles on existing pages, not for writing six more guides.

**B0 blog meta rewrite verdict: no material CTR lift.** 28-day CTRs vs the May baseline: startup-costs 0.25% (was 0.26%), paper-napkins 0.10% (was 0.16%, impressions exploded to 10,678/28d), pizza-boxes 0.09% (was 0.20%), smoothie-cups 0.58% (was 0.43%), catering 0.21% (was 0.06%, tiny base). Two up, three flat-to-down. The rewritten titles did not fix CTR; the impression mix on these pages is increasingly AI fan-out queries that never click. Backlog B0 should be closed as measured-and-neutral; no more effort on blog meta CTR.
