## 2. Category `napkins` buying_guide: new section prepended

```markdown
## Paper Napkins for Restaurants and Cafés

Choose by service style rather than by price alone:

- Cafés and coffee shops: 1-ply dispenser napkins are the cheapest per guest and cut waste, because guests take one at a time. Our unbleached dispenser napkins come with two free dispensers.
- Casual dining and takeaway: 2-ply 33 x 33cm napkins, white or unbleached, cover most table and counter service. Unbleached kraft signals eco-friendly without costing more.
- Table-service restaurants: 8-fold 40 x 40cm airlaid dinner napkins feel close to linen with no laundry bill. Pocket napkins hold cutlery for pre-set tables.
- Bars and events: cocktail napkins in white, black or bamboo pulp for drinks and canapés.

As a rule of thumb, allow one to two napkins per cover for table service and two to three per order for takeaway and counter service, then size your cases from weekly covers. Free samples are available if you want to compare ply and airlaid before committing to a case.
```
