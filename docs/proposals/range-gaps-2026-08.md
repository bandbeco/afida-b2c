# Range Gaps: What Search Demand Says We Don't Sell (August 2026)

For Afida leadership. Source: the [August 2026 Ahrefs extraction](/seo/ahrefs-extraction-2026-08-18.md). We now have the full list of UK searches our direct competitors capture and we cannot, because the products are not in the range. These are near-zero-competition searches (difficulty 0-2 out of 100); where we stock the product, the SEO team can build the page. Where we don't, ranking is impossible and this is a stocking decision, not an SEO one.

Verified against the live catalogue on 2026-08-19: none of the lines below have a single active product.

## The gaps, by monthly UK search demand

**1. Cake, cupcake and bakery boxes: ~12,000 searches/mo. Recommended.**
"cake boxes" alone is 5,700/mo at difficulty 2; the cluster adds cake slice boxes, cupcake boxes, cookie boxes, bakery packaging. This is the single largest gap we found, it is bakery/café demand that matches our existing coffee-shop customer base, and the competitor winning it (a general catering-disposables shop) ranks with an ordinary category page. Kraft/recyclable cake boxes fit the eco positioning cleanly. Candidate for the own-brand import route or an existing supplier line.

**2. Custom-print service beyond coffee cups: ~1,500/mo of explicit demand, plus the highest commercial value per click in the dataset. Recommended.**
We already sell branded coffee cups and stock pre-printed greaseproof designs. Searchers also want custom greaseproof paper, printed/custom paper bags, and personalised pizza boxes; advertisers pay £1.20-£2.80 per click on these terms, the strongest buying-intent signal we measured. A competitor at our exact domain strength built a whole branded-packaging section on this demand and ranks for the generic head terms with it. Extending the print service to greaseproof, bags and pizza boxes is a service decision (supplier print capability), not a stocking one.

**3. Chicken boxes and buckets: ~1,000/mo. Cheap adjacency, worth considering.**
Compostable chicken boxes and fried-chicken buckets. We already sell burger boxes and fish & chip boxes; this completes the hot-fast-food set. The winner here is a DR 10 site.

**4. Paper table covers and banquet rolls: ~2,000/mo. Minor.**
Paper tablecloths, banquet roll. Event/catering demand, brand-compatible (paper), but lower value and further from the core range.

**5. Kitchen foil and cling film: ~4,700/mo. Recommend declining.**
High volume and competitors sell them as catering basics, but cling film in particular contradicts the eco positioning the brand is built on. Only worth revisiting as trade-account basket completion, not for search traffic.

**6. Seasonal napkin designs: ~2,500/mo, seasonal. Note only.**
"christmas napkins" peaks at 2,500/mo at difficulty 0. We stock plain napkins only. A seasonal printed range would capture Q4 demand; shelf-life risk is the trade-off.

## What we're asking

A yes/no/later on items 1-3. On a yes, the SEO side ships the category page the moment stock exists (the page template and internal-linking machinery are already being built for the in-range work), and items enter the usual velocity-based import evaluation. Nothing here blocks the in-range SEO work already underway.
