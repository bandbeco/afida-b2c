## 1. Headline

August 2026 is the best calendar month on record: **£5,077 / 46 payments / 4,964 visitors**. The last-30-day window that ends today does **not** continue that rate. Visitors are up 85% on the prior 30 days while payments are only up 8%, because the extra traffic is Direct/display/bot junk that does not buy. UK conversion is flat. Returning visitors (23% of traffic) still produce 90% of the orders.

The [H2 marketing strategy](/reports/marketing-strategy-2026-h2.md) said paid ads showed £0 in Afida's own analytics. That is no longer true: `googlesyndication.com` last-touched **£1,023 on 2 payments**, while `doubleclick.net` sent 367 visitors and £0. Pillar 1 (ads accountability) finally has a scoreboard, and it says display is mostly waste plus two fat orders.

## 8. What to do with this

1. **Reconcile August with the ads agency (Pillar 1, now possible).** Display is no longer £0 in our analytics. Ask them to split August by network: Search/Shopping vs Display/PMax vs YouTube. Our independent scoreboard: ~575 display-cluster visitors, 2 payments, £1,023 last-touched, plus a 22 Aug Direct+Shopping dump of random SKUs at £0. If spend is still ~£500, those two Feel-Good orders may pay the month on last-touch and still be the wrong mix. The 367 doubleclick visitors at 97% bounce should come off.

2. **Stop sending paid traffic to aluminium, liners, and oddball SKUs.** `no-9-deep` / `no-6a` / `8l-completely-liner` / `cold-cup-89-series` are the volume landings and £0. Constrain the Merchant feed / PMax asset groups to the lines that actually sell (hot cups, Bella pots, the commercial categories). This is the same SKU-oddity the July audits flagged; paid has now amplified it.

3. **Compound returning visitors, not first-click volume.** 23% of people, 90% of orders. Named continuity offers from the B2B-offers note, Klaviyo post-purchase/reorder, and not buying more display. First visits almost never convert.

4. **Do not read the conversion-rate drop as a CRO emergency.** UK conversion is flat at ~0.9%. Headline 0.79% is mix. Mac/Safari already converts at 3.5%. Chasing Android/Chrome conversion of this traffic is chasing bots and display.

5. **Leave informational blog investment paused.** 405 visitors, 97.5% bounce, £0, and startup-costs is no longer the #1 URL. Product and feed traffic is the new top of funnel; it needs feed quality, not more guides.

6. **Watch September weekly payments.** W33 16 → W36 4. If W37 (this week, 3 payments on 7 Sep already) does not recover toward ~8–10, August was a spike not a new baseline. Next Datafast pull ~2026-10-08, alongside the ~09-15 GSC checkpoint already committed.
