# SEO Audit — afida.com — 2026-07-02

Sources: Datafast API (site analytics), Google Search Console export (last 3 months, web search, to 2026-07-02), Betterstack production logs, production database, live-site technical checks.

## Headline findings

1. **URGENT: the June category restructure left ~15 ranking category URLs returning 404 with no redirects.** These pages held ~16k impressions/quarter, including the site's best-performing category page, and GSC already shows impressions down ~15-20% and position slipping since June 24. These are exactly the commercial pages the SEO programme (Move 2) is trying to lift.
2. **Organic traffic is up sharply**: Google-referred visitors nearly tripled month over month (648 → 1,759). Revenue up 61% (£1,828 → £2,944). GSC shows weekly clicks compounding from ~25 to ~80 since April, with brand share of clicks falling from 62% to 55% (non-brand growing).
3. **Purchase-goal tracking is still losing most conversions** (3 tracked vs 31 real payments in 30 days), even though the API-key 401 is resolved and the pageview gate shipped today.
4. Blog content dominates traffic but converts £0 attributed revenue, unchanged from the June baseline.

## 1. Urgent: category rename 404s (no redirects)

Category slugs were renamed in production around 2026-06-22 to 2026-06-25 (per `categories.updated_at`). The old URLs, which Google still ranks and sends traffic to, now return **404 Not Found**. There are no `UrlRedirect` records and no route-level redirects for them (the route-level legacy map in `config/routes.rb` only covers the ancient `/category/*` paths).

That is roughly **230 organic visits per 30 days landing on 404s (~7% of all traffic, and a much larger share of commercial category traffic)**. Every day these stay 404, Google reads the pages as gone and rankings that were "stuck on page 2-3" will start dropping out entirely, undoing Move 2 work.

**Action (this week, highest priority): 301 redirects for every old path above.** Two options:
- Route-level redirect map, same pattern as the existing `/category/*` block in `config/routes.rb`.
- A durable fix: persist slug history on Category (e.g. a `slug_history`/friendly_id-style table) so every future admin rename auto-redirects. June proved renames happen casually in admin; this will recur.

Also worth doing after redirects: resubmit the sitemap in GSC to speed up recrawl.

- **401 Invalid API Key on goal writes: RESOLVED.** Zero occurrences in the last 12 days of logs; goals are being created again (funnel below view_item is populated: add_to_cart 24, begin_checkout 12, purchase 3 in 30d).
- **Pageview-gate fix (master 6e84e9f9 + 42d94e1f): DEPLOYED today** (prod runs 7f19aaea). Remaining `[DataFast] FAILED ... no recorded pageviews` 404s (42 on June 30 alone, mostly bot add-to-carts) should now become quiet "[DataFast] skipped" lines. Watch logs to confirm.
- **Purchase goal still catching only ~10% of purchases: OPEN.** Last 30d: 3 `purchase` goals vs 31 real payments. Last 7d: 0 tracked vs 6 real payments, while only 1 purchase FAILED line appeared in logs since June 29. So most purchases are neither tracked nor failing loudly; they are being dropped silently, most likely blank `datafast_visitor_id` on the webhook path (the subscriber silently skips blank-visitor goals). Needs a dedicated investigation: log the skip reason, and check whether `datafast_visitor_id` is reaching Stripe metadata / the Order for real purchases.
- Consequence for this audit: Datafast page/referrer/device revenue attribution only reflects the ~1/3 of payments that carry a visitor id; treat attributed-revenue splits as directional only.

## 6. Google Search Console (last 3 months to 2026-07-02)

### Trend: steady organic build, with an early wobble after the renames
Weekly clicks grew from ~25 (early April) to ~80 (mid-June); weekly impressions from 4.3k to a 21k peak the week of June 15. The growth is a gradual impressions build, not a single event, and it corroborates the Datafast late-May inflection: rankings/impressions have been compounding since April.

Since the June 24 renames, settled daily data shows impressions down ~15-20% from the mid-June level (~3,100-3,200/day → ~2,400-2,700/day) and average position worsening from ~10-11 to ~13-14.6. Early 404 decay is visible but not yet catastrophic; Google typically takes weeks to fully drop 404s, so fast 301s can still rescue most of the equity.

### The 404'd URLs were carrying real search equity
Over the 3-month window, the now-dead category URLs earned **55 clicks and 15,692 impressions**. Standouts:
- `/categories/cups-and-drinks/ice-cream-cups` — 28 clicks / 6,973 impressions / position 12.8. This was the **single best-performing category page on the site** (and #3 page overall by clicks). It is currently a 404.
- `/categories/hot-food/soup-containers` — 2,382 impressions (a Move 2 target, now 404).
- `/categories/cups-and-drinks/straws` — 1,497 imp; `/categories/tableware/aluminium-containers` — 1,317 imp; `/categories/hot-food/bagasse-containers` — 886 imp; `/categories/hot-food/pizza-boxes` — 740 imp.

Meanwhile the new URLs (`/categories/cups-and-accessories/*`, `/categories/food-containers/*`) are essentially absent from GSC: Google hasn't discovered/re-ranked them yet. Without 301s they start from zero.

### Query mix: improving but still the same shape
- Brand share of clicks: **55%**, down from 62% at the June 18 snapshot; non-brand is growing faster than brand. Good.
- Blog still dominates impressions (74%, unchanged) and now takes 53% of clicks.
- Commercial head terms remain page 2-3: "ice cream cups" pos 11.7 (1,700 imp), "sustainable food packaging" pos 12.9 (1,454 imp), "eco friendly food packaging" pos 24.7 (1,018 imp).
- "sustainable packaging for restaurants" held at pos 2.7, still the B2B template to replicate.
- Move 2 target pages (3-month averages, mostly pre-dating the June 19 ship): natureflex-bags 16.8, coffee-shops 11.2, vegware 10.7, restaurants 17.4, cutlery 40.4. The formal before/after read stays scheduled for ~July 17.
- Product snippets rich results: 132 clicks / 28.9k impressions — structured data is pulling weight.
- US remains ~25% of impressions at position 7.1 but 0.1% CTR (can't buy); UK position averages 16.5, confirming commercial UK rankings are the constraint.

### GSC implication for the priority list
The redirect fix (action 1) is now quantified: the dead URLs held ~16k impressions/quarter including the best category page on the site, new URLs are unindexed, and sitewide position is already slipping. Ship the 301s, then request reindexing of the biggest old URLs (ice-cream-cups, soup-containers, straws, aluminium-containers) in GSC.
