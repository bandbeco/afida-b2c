# Afida Shop Knowledge Bundle

OKF bundle for the Afida shop project. Conventions live in the repo `CLAUDE.md` under "docs/ knowledge bundle". Only `seo/` has been retrofitted with frontmatter and an index so far; other directories are pending (Phase 2), so treat their docs' status as unknown until marked.

# Areas

* [SEO](/seo/index.md) - Organic growth strategy, backlog, keyword research, and shipped content drafts. Curated.
* Plans (`/plans/`) - Dated feature and design plans from 2025-01 onwards. Not yet retrofitted; many are shipped or abandoned but unmarked.
* Reports (`/reports/`) - Growth plans, marketing strategy, bestsellers analysis, monthly timesheets.
* Proposals (`/proposals/`) - Marketing partnership proposal and its implementation annexes.
* Audits (`/audits/`) - Lighthouse and accessibility audit outputs, mostly JSON/HTML artifacts.
* Articles (`/articles/`) - Long-form article drafts for the shop.
* Insights (`/insights/`) - Research notes.
* Superpowers (`/superpowers/`) - Plans and specs from superpowers-workflow sessions.
* Invoices (`/invoices/`) - Private, gitignored.

# Key root documents

* [Developer guide](/developer_guide.md) - Codebase orientation for developers.
* [Analytics strategy](/analytics-strategy.md) - Analytics and measurement strategy.
* [Worklog](/worklog.md) - Non-code work sessions; feeds the monthly timesheet.
