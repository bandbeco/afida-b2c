# Afida Shop Knowledge Bundle

OKF bundle for the Afida shop project. Conventions live in the repo `CLAUDE.md` under "docs/ knowledge bundle". Curated areas (frontmatter, indexes, linted in CI): `seo/`, `plans/`, `reports/`, `proposals/`, `runbooks/`. The rest is pending retrofit; treat those docs' status as unknown until marked.

# Curated Areas

* [SEO](/seo/index.md) - Organic growth strategy, backlog, keyword research, and shipped content drafts.
* [Plans](/plans/index.md) - Dated feature and design plans from late 2025 onwards, each marked shipped, abandoned, superseded or active.
* [Reports](/reports/index.md) - Growth plan, marketing strategy, point-in-time analyses, timesheets.
* [Proposals](/proposals/index.md) - The 2025 partnership package and its annexes (superseded).
* [Runbooks](/runbooks/index.md) - Deploy, credentials, Stripe and Datafast operational knowledge.

# Not Yet Retrofitted

* ADRs (`/adr/`) - Architecture decision records (started 2026-07 with the on-site checkout decision).
* Audits (`/audits/`) - Lighthouse and accessibility audit outputs, mostly JSON/HTML artifacts.
* Articles (`/articles/`) - Long-form article drafts for the shop.
* Insights (`/insights/`) - Research notes.
* Superpowers (`/superpowers/`) - Plans and specs from superpowers-workflow sessions.
* Invoices (`/invoices/`) - Private, gitignored.
* Most root-level docs (developer guide, PRD, strategy docs, worklogs).

# Key root documents

* [Developer guide](/developer_guide.md) - Codebase orientation for developers.
* [Analytics strategy](/analytics-strategy.md) - Analytics and measurement strategy.
* Worklogs (`/worklog.md`, `/worklog-auto.md`) - Non-code work sessions feeding the monthly timesheet; gitignored, local only.
